VERDANTIA

WORLD 1 — THE SEED · WORLD 2 — THE EMBER
You awaken on a mossy island above an endless sea of clouds, a cracked metal hammer in hand. Beside you stands a village: Hollowreach. Mira the scavenger knows where the Seed Fragment is hidden — deep in the Heartwood, guarded by the ancient Rootwarden.

To the west lies Pyroclast, the volcanic realm of The Ember — rivers of lava, blackstone fortresses, and the legendary forges of the Ember Clans. At its heart, the Molten King guards the Ember Fragment.

Mine blocks, craft the 96 world weapons, and hunt the 5 guardians — the four of Verdantia and the Molten King of Pyroclast. Defeat the final guardians, and the Seed and the Ember will enter your hammer — both worlds will heal.
