VERDANTIA

WORLD 1 — THE SEED · THE WORLD THAT REMEMBERS
You awaken on a mossy island above an endless sea of clouds, a cracked metal hammer in hand. Beside you stands a village: Hollowreach. Mira the scavenger knows where the Seed Fragment is hidden — deep in the Heartwood, guarded by the ancient Rootwarden.

Mine blocks, craft the 48 Verdant weapons, and hunt the 4 guardians of the deep forest. Defeat the final guardian, and the Seed will enter your hammer — Verdantia will heal.
