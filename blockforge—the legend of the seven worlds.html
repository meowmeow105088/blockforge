CHAPTER V · The Kingdom Beneath the Leaves Long before the Great Shattering, Verdantia was a paradise of endless forests, singing rivers, and a great tree called the Heartwood that touched the sky. Its roots drank from the Worldcore and its leaves breathed life into every creature beneath them. The Hollowreach village nestled in the Heartwood's shadow, its people the last keepers of the old Blockbinding arts.

But the Shattering corrupted the world. Black tendrils of the Null crept through the roots, turning living wood to obsidian. The Heartwood's glow dimmed. The people of Hollowreach fled, and the great tree stood alone, slowly dying.

You are the first to arrive in a thousand years. The old woman Mira still waits in Hollowreach, tending the last embers of the Binding. She knows the truth: deep beneath the Heartwood's roots, something ancient stirs. The Rootwarden — a guardian turned by corruption — has awakened. It must be stopped, or Verdantia will fall silent forever.
